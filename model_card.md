# ProFair Observability model card

## Intended use

Aggregate scientific analysis of trade-fair market observability.

## Prohibited use

- individual identity determination;
- employment or personnel decisions;
- evaluation of individual professional worth;
- publication of identifiable records;
- treating name-based gender classification as a definitive individual fact.

## Primary output

A multidimensional profile. The optional composite score is secondary and
must not replace dimension-level results.

## Current limitations

- administrative contacts do not necessarily establish physical attendance;
- organisation proxies may differ from validated legal entities;
- gender classifications may be uncertain or incomplete;
- job-title classification requires manual and inter-coder validation;
- exhibition area is a partial proxy for materialised presence;
- external benchmarks may not match the fair population exactly;
- the initial empirical design contains ten purposively selected IFEMA MADRID
  fair editions and does not constitute a random sample of the exhibition
  industry.
